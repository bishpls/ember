# EMBER III · KINDLE

**Logline:** She has survived the wolves and the alpha, but the void doesn't end. It rises as a single colossus that eclipses the moon and crushes her into the snow. With her last ember guttering, she remembers what she is, and she *ignites*.

**Runtime:** 72.0s at 150 BPM: beat = 0.4s, bar = 1.6s, 45 bars. Picture is 24 fps and animated **on twos/threes** with 24 fps bursts for smears. The score is my own synth, beat-locked.

**Palette arc:**
- **Act 1:** indigo/blue-black and moonlight silver, with ember orange as the *only* warm colour.
- **Eclipse:** the palette drains to near-monochrome, and the ember becomes the only colour in the frame.
- **Ignition:** a hard flip to TRIGGER-hot orange, magenta and gold, with the colours inverted on impact frames.
- **Dawn:** pale gold and rose sky, soft white snow turning to falling embers.

**Motif:** the ember. It's a spark in her cloak's clasp and in her eyes. It dims at the low point, then blooms into wildfire.

**Character (she gets a face for the first time):** she's about 19 and lean. She has choppy silver-white hair with one ember-red streak and ember-orange eyes that glow when she fights. She wears a crimson hooded half-cloak with a torn hem (black lining), a black fitted combat suit with leather straps, knee boots with steel caps, fingerless gloves and a charcoal scarf. The ember clasp at her collar glows. Her weapon, **Kindle**, is a folding gun-scythe: a black shaft with crimson stripes, a rifle receiver and a wide curved blade whose edge glows faint orange.

**The Void:** ink-black wolves with torn-shadow manes, white-blue glowing eyes and smoke trailing off them. The **Hollow King** is a mountain-sized wolf formed from the swarm. It has dozens of eyes and a crown of broken black spikes, and it eclipses the moon.

## Production method

- **Designs** (Nano Banana Pro): a character sheet, a Void sheet and a style frame. Every keyframe is then generated *with the sheets as reference images* to keep her consistent.
- **Keyframes:** 1–2 per shot, painted to key-visual quality.
- **Motion:** image-to-video on Veo 3.1 (Lite for exploration, Fast or Standard for hero shots), using first and last frames where the action must land exactly.
- **Anime-ification pass:** retime to 12/8 fps holds with 24 fps smear bursts, run a line/cel clean-up grade, and composite my own FX. The FX are impact frames, speed lines, radial smears, embers, flashes, shake and chromatic aberration.
- **Quiet shots:** parallax camera moves on painted keys.
- **Edit:** beat-locked to the synth score; every seam has a transition.
